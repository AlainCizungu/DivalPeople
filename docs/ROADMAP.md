# Dival People — Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundation
Documentation, repository, environments, CI/CD, design system, security baseline, and architecture decisions.

## Phase 1 — Platform
Tenants, authentication, roles, organization structure, users, audit, English/French, notifications, files, dashboard.

**Exit:** two isolated tenants, no cross-tenant access, bilingual UI, security tests passing.

## Phase 2 — Core HR
Employees, departments, branches, contracts, documents, dependents, emergency contacts, history, and expiration alerts.

## Phase 3 — Recruitment and onboarding
Requisitions, candidates, interviews, evaluations, offers, approvals, onboarding, probation, offboarding.

## Phase 4 — Time, performance, and learning
Leave, balances, approvals, attendance, shifts, overtime, goals, reviews, courses, certifications.

## Phase 5 — Payroll preparation
Compensation, payroll periods, allowances, deductions, bonuses, approvals, payslips, bank/accounting exports, analytics.

## Phase 6 — Employee self-service
Mobile portal, profile, leave, documents, payslips, benefits, HR requests, notifications, AI assistant.

## Phase 7 — Financial services
Partners, products, consent, salary advances, credit, insurance, savings, payroll repayment, partner APIs.

**Exit:** one sandbox bank and insurer integration with complete consent and application flow.

## Phase 8 — Fraud intelligence
Duplicate identities/bank accounts, ghost-employee indicators, payroll anomalies, salary overrides, conflicts of interest, investigations, risk dashboard.

## Phase 9 — Industry editions
- Banking: insider risk, access review, compliance, mandatory leave
- NGO: grants, projects, consultants, donor reports, safeguarding
- Telecom: field workforce and optional TIX integration
- Government: public-sector workflows and audit controls
- Healthcare: credentials and clinical workforce compliance

## Phase 10 — Advanced AI
Turnover prediction, workforce forecasts, executive summaries, network analysis, document intelligence, and skills intelligence.

## First-year plan
- Q1: platform foundation, core HR, bilingual support
- Q2: recruitment, onboarding, leave, self-service
- Q3: performance, learning, payroll, analytics
- Q4: financial-services and fraud pilots, first industry edition, first design partner

## Commercial milestones
Prototype → design partner → paid pilot → first annual subscription → second industry edition → multi-country expansion.
