# Dival People — Product Requirements

## Product
Dival People is a bilingual, AI-enabled HR, workforce intelligence, employee financial services, and fraud-prevention platform built on the Dival Intelligence Platform (DIP).

## Target customers
Banks, NGOs, telecoms, insurers, healthcare organizations, government agencies, schools, mining/logistics firms, and multi-country employers with roughly 100–5,000 workers.

## Product principles
- Multi-tenant SaaS
- English and French from launch
- Security and auditability by default
- Mobile-friendly employee self-service
- Configurable rather than heavily customized
- AI is advisory; humans make high-impact decisions
- Financial services are delivered through regulated partners

## Personas
- Employee
- Line manager
- HR administrator
- HR manager
- Recruiter
- Payroll/finance officer
- Compliance officer
- Executive
- Tenant administrator
- Platform administrator

## Core modules
1. Organization structure: legal entities, branches, departments, locations, cost centers, reporting lines.
2. Employee records: profiles, contracts, job history, compensation, dependents, emergency contacts, IDs, credentials, documents, transfers, promotions.
3. Recruitment: requisitions, candidates, applications, interviews, evaluations, offers, approvals.
4. Onboarding/offboarding: checklists, document collection, equipment, policy acknowledgment, training, probation, exit clearance, access removal.
5. Leave and attendance: policies, balances, requests, approvals, shifts, overtime, absence tracking, country holidays.
6. Performance and learning: goals, reviews, probation, skills, training, certifications, succession.
7. Payroll preparation: salary, allowances, deductions, bonuses, overtime, approval, payslips, bank/accounting exports, history.
8. Employee self-service: profile, leave, payslips, documents, benefits, requests, training, notifications.
9. Employee financial services: salary advances, earned-wage access, affordable credit, health insurance, savings, pensions, financial education, partner integrations, consent.
10. Fraud intelligence: ghost employees, duplicate identities, shared bank accounts, unusual salary changes, payroll overrides, expense anomalies, employee/vendor links, departed-user access.
11. AI assistant: policy Q&A, document retrieval, HR request guidance, contract alerts, workforce summaries, executive reports.
12. Analytics: headcount, turnover, absenteeism, payroll cost, recruitment, contracts, training, performance, financial-service usage, fraud trends.

## Industry editions
### Banking
Screening, compliance training, segregation-of-duties review, insider-risk indicators, mandatory leave, access reviews, payroll anomalies, certification tracking.

### NGO
Grant-funded staffing, project allocations, consultant management, donor timesheets, safeguarding, field offices, per diem/travel, contract alerts.

### Telecom
Field workforce, branch operations, shifts, compliance, employee fraud controls, optional TIX integration.

### Government
Public-sector structures, personnel records, controlled approvals, audit evidence, workforce reporting.

### Healthcare
Credential tracking, shifts, clinical compliance training, workforce planning.

## MVP
- Tenant, user, role, and organization management
- Employee records, documents, and contracts
- Recruitment and onboarding
- Leave and basic attendance
- Performance reviews
- Payroll preparation
- Employee self-service
- English/French
- Audit logging
- Basic dashboards
- Basic fraud alerts
- Partner-ready financial-service workflows

## MVP non-goals
- Full statutory payroll for every African country
- Dival acting as lender or insurer
- Autonomous hiring, fraud, discipline, credit, or insurance decisions
- Complex biometric attendance
- Full ERP
- Premature microservices

## Success measures
Monthly active users, employee self-service adoption, HR processing time, payroll accuracy, contract compliance, training completion, alert resolution, onboarding time, annual recurring revenue, customer retention.
